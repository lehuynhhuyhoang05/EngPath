# EngPath content quality rubric

## Blocking checks

Any failure below results in `reject` or `revise`:

- English is incorrect or unnatural for the intended lesson.
- More than one option is defensibly correct.
- The declared answer is wrong.
- The Vietnamese explanation teaches an incorrect rule.
- The item requires knowledge outside the declared grade or prerequisite path without warning.
- Source or ownership is misrepresented.
- A pronunciation claim lacks a valid acoustic or linguistic basis.

## Scored checks

Score each category from 0–2:

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Incorrect | Mostly correct | Correct and precise |
| Clarity | Confusing | Understandable | Concise and unambiguous |
| Pedagogy | No learning value | Practises recall | Explains transferable reasoning |
| Grade fit | Misaligned | Borderline | Appropriate |
| Distractors | Broken | Usable | Plausible and diagnostic |
| Vietnamese support | Misleading | Literal/basic | Natural and corrective |
| Metadata | Missing | Partial | Complete and traceable |

Publishing recommendation:

- `pass`: no blocking issue and at least 12/14;
- `revise`: no fundamental flaw but one or more corrections are required;
- `reject`: a blocking issue undermines the learning objective or provenance.

## Independent answer check

For objective questions:

1. Hide or ignore the declared answer.
2. Solve from the prompt and options.
3. Compare with the declared answer.
4. Test whether another option can be defended.
5. Verify the explanation matches the reason the answer is correct.

For pronunciation prompts, verify the target sound actually occurs in the text and that the remediation describes that sound rather than a spelling rule.
